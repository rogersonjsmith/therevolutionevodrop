Understanding Traditional Water Softeners

How Do Traditional Water Softeners Work?

Traditional water softeners use a process known as ion exchange. As hard water passes through a tank filled with resin beads, calcium and magnesium ions are exchanged for sodium or potassium ions.

This process effectively removes hardness minerals from the water, resulting in softer water throughout the home.

Although this technology has been widely used for many years, it comes with ongoing maintenance requirements, including salt refills and regeneration cycles.

Common Features of Traditional Water Softeners

  • Remove calcium and magnesium from water
  • Require regular salt replenishment
  • Use regeneration cycles
  • Produce wastewater during regeneration
  • Require routine maintenance

What Makes EVODROP Different?

A New Approach to Water Treatment

Unlike conventional systems, EVODROP does not rely on removing hardness minerals from the water. Instead, it conditions the water by influencing how minerals behave, reducing their tendency to form hard deposits on surfaces.

This modern approach helps minimize limescale while allowing beneficial minerals to remain in the water.

Rather than changing the chemical composition of water, EVODROP focuses on optimizing its physical characteristics.
